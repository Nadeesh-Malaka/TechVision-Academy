Who We Are

About TechVision IT Academy

Since 2010, we have been on a mission to make world-class technology education accessible to every aspiring professional in Sri Lanka.

Our Story

From a Small Lab to Sri Lanka's #1 IT Academy

TechVision IT Academy was founded in 2010 by a group of passionate software engineers and educators who saw a critical gap between university graduates and the skills demanded by the technology industry.

What started as a modest 20-seat computer lab in Colombo 03 has grown into a full-scale institution with three campuses, over 35 expert instructors, and more than 5,200 alumni working at top companies in Sri Lanka, the UK, Australia, and beyond.

Today, TechVision is recognised as the leading private IT training institution in the country, awarded the "Best IT Education Provider" title at the National Education Excellence Awards 2023 and 2024.

Our Direction

Mission & Vision

The principles that guide every decision we make and every course we design.

🚀

Our Mission

To deliver accessible, affordable, and industry aligned IT education that equips learners with the practical skills, mindset, and confidence to thrive in the global technology economy. We are committed to bridging the gap between academic knowledge and workplace readiness through project-based learning and mentorship.

🔭

Our Vision

To become the most trusted IT education institution in South Asia by 2030 recognised for producing highly skilled, ethical, and innovative technology professionals who contribute meaningfully to society and drive digital transformation across industries in Sri Lanka and beyond.
